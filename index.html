The story beyond the label

Every garment carries a story.

Threadline is exploring a designer-controlled Cultural Provenance Passport that brings the anatomy, meaning, cultural context, authorship and craftsmanship of a garment into the shopping experience.

A garment is more than what it is made of. It carries the decisions, skills, references and people that brought it into being.

Why Threadline

Make the invisible visible.

Product pages tell shoppers what an item is. Threadline explores how those pages could also communicate why it matters, through structured information controlled and approved by the designer.

A working prototype

Designed to be tested.

  1. 01 Place a passport on one product page.
  2. 02 Invite shoppers to explore the garment's story.
  3. 03 Measure opens, engagement and perceived value.
  4. 04 Learn with a designer before scaling.
